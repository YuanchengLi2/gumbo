We tried a four-day week for six months. Here's what happened.

In January our twelve-person studio moved to a four-day week with no cut in pay. We kept Fridays off and protected Monday mornings for deep work, with no meetings before noon.

Output held steady: we shipped 31 projects against 29 the half-year before. The biggest change was in sick days, which fell by almost half, and in how few people checked messages on their day off.

The hard part was clients. Two expected same-day answers on Fridays, and we lost one of them.
