Sony WH-1000XM6 review: the quietest headphones we've tested

Sony's sixth-generation flagship keeps the fold-flat design, adds a twelve-microphone array, and lasts 32 hours on a charge. At $449 it is expensive, but noise cancelling on planes and trains is a clear step above the Bose QuietComfort Ultra.

Verdict: the best noise-cancelling headphones for frequent travellers. Best price we found this week: $399 at most big retailers.
