Commercial lease agreement

This lease is made between Mission Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Park Studio LLC ("Tenant") for the premises at 2150 Bryant Street, Unit 3, San Francisco, California.

1. Term

The term of this lease is twenty-four months. The Tenant may renew the lease for one further term of twelve months by giving written notice no later than ninety days before the end of the term. Any holdover after the end of the term shall be month to month at one hundred and fifty percent of the last monthly rent.

2. Use of premises

The premises shall be used for photography, video production and related office work only. The Tenant shall comply with all laws, ordinances and building rules that apply to the premises and its use, and shall not cause any nuisance to other tenants of the building.

3. Maintenance and repairs

The Landlord shall keep the roof, structure, and building systems in good repair. The Tenant shall keep the interior of the premises clean and in good condition, and shall promptly report any damage, leak or failure of building systems to the Landlord in writing.

4. Insurance

The Tenant shall carry general liability insurance with limits of no less than one million dollars per occurrence, naming the Landlord as an additional insured, and shall provide a certificate of insurance before taking possession.

5. Alterations

The Tenant shall make no structural alterations without the Landlord's prior written consent. Non-structural improvements, such as lighting grids, backdrop rails and paint, are permitted provided they are removed and the premises restored at the end of the term.

6. Access

The Landlord may enter the premises with twenty-four hours' notice for inspection, repairs, or to show the premises to prospective tenants or lenders, and at any time in an emergency.

7. Rent and deposit

Monthly rent is $3,850, due on the first of each month. The security deposit of $7,700 is due by March 3, 2027, together with the first month's rent, before keys are released.
