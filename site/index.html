How it works

Say it. Watch it. Ship it.

No timeline wrangling, no keyframe hunting. Figjam does the editing; you stay in charge.

1

Add your footage

Drop in a talk, a podcast, a vlog or a pile of clips. Figjam watches it and reads every word.

2

Say what you want

Describe the video the way you'd brief an editor.

“Make the middle faster and punch in when I make a key point.”
3

Watch it edit, live

Cuts, titles, effects and music appear on the timeline as Figjam works. Drag, trim or ask for changes — then export.

What it does

Everything an editor does. In minutes.

Cuts

Finds the good parts

Cuts the rambles, pauses and filler, keeps your best lines, and opens with a hook.

Motion graphics

Graphics made for your video

Chapter titles, lower thirds and animated cards designed for what you're actually saying.

Captions

Captions, timed to the word

Clean, readable captions that follow your speech exactly.

Music

An original score

Music composed for your edit that sits under your voice, never over it.

Effects

Punch-ins, zooms and grades

Emphasis where it matters and a consistent look from start to finish.

You're in control

A real timeline

Every change lands as a block you can see, move and trim. Undo anything.

Built for creators with great footage and no time to edit.

Hours of editing become a conversation. Spend your time making things, not scrubbing timelines.
