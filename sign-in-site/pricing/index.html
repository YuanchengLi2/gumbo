Questions about paying

What uses credits?

Only the AI's work on your requests: watching and listening to footage, planning the edit, writing titles and graphics. Editing by hand, playback and export never use credits.

What happens when I run out?

Gumbo tells you, and you can keep editing by hand. Your credits refill at the start of the next month, or you can add 500 for $10 any time.

Do credits roll over?

A plan's monthly credits refresh each month. Credits you buy on top never expire.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Any time, from Gumbo's settings. A new plan starts right away, and Stripe charges only the difference for the rest of the month.
