What to ask

Big, small, or vague.

There's no right way to ask. Here's the kind of thing people type.

Tiny tweaks

The fiddly stuff that takes ten clicks.

Trim two frames off the end.Nudge the title up a bit.Make the logo smaller. No, smaller.

Fixes

Point at the problem the way you'd describe it.

Cut every um and long pause.The audio dips at 0:40.Lose the bit where the dog barks.

Looks

Describe a feeling, not a filter.

Like a 90s camcorder.Warmer, like the last day of summer.Make the drone shots match my phone.

Things no template has

Graphics, titles and effects are made from scratch for your request.

Draw our route from Hanoi to Ha Long.A countdown before the drop.Price tags that pop up next to each dish.

The whole thing

Hand over a folder and a sentence.

Turn my 40-minute talk into three Shorts.A 60-second trailer for our trip.Use what I shot today.

Vague is fine too

Don't know what you want yet? Say so. Gumbo makes a call, tells you what it changed, and you can undo any piece of it.

How it can do anything

It doesn't pick from a menu. It makes the edit.

1

It watches and listens

Gumbo knows every shot and every word in your footage, so you can point at things the way you'd describe them: "the part where she laughs."

2

It builds what's missing

Titles, maps, graphics and effects are built for your request instead of pulled from a template library. Music is composed for your cut, or found free to use.

3

It lands on your timeline

Every change is a block you can drag, trim or undo. Keep talking until it's right, or take over by hand.
