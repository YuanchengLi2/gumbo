Editing by hand

A typical evening
  1. 6:30Import everything and scrub through it, twice.
  2. 7:45A rough cut. It's too long.
  3. 8:40Still hunting for music that doesn't clash.
  4. 9:55Typing titles and captions, one by one.
  5. 11:20Export. Notice a typo. Export again.

Five hours. Dinner went cold.

With Gumbo

Same evening
  1. 7:00Toss in your clips. Type one sentence.
  2. 7:13The cut is ready: titles, grade and an original score.
  3. 7:15"Make the intro snappier." Done.
  4. 7:18Export.
  5. 7:19Posted. Dinner's still hot.
